How do you brew?

Pick one and we will bring forward the bag that suits it.

    From bean to cup

    Seven moments between the coffee tree and the cup on your table, and the part of each one that happens in Erbil.

    It starts on a tree

    Arabica cherries ripen on the coffee tree. We buy the green seed from the world’s finest origins.

    Then it crosses the mountains

    Green coffee travels to Kurdistan and up to Erbil, where it rests before roasting.

    It arrives under the citadel

    Our roastery stands at 32 Park on the Erbil–Mosul road, in a city that has been lived in for millennia.

    We roast to an Italian profile

    Each blend has a curve set with Italian coffee masters, from a light filter roast to a deep espresso.

    In a drum, a lot at a time

    Beans turn in a hot drum until the roaster calls the end of the roast, then cool fast on the tray.

    Sealed in its colour

    Every blend has its own bag, so the shelf reads at a glance: black for Intenso, blue for Premium.

    And ends in your cup

    At home, in your café, or on a Rocket machine we set up for you.

    Machines and grinders

    Espresso machines from Milan

    We carry Rocket Milano machines for the home bar and the café counter, and we help you choose the one that fits your cups per day.

    Grinders that hold a dose

    Mahlkönig and Ditting grinders, from the E65S grind-by-weight for espresso to all-round grinders for filter and Turkish.
